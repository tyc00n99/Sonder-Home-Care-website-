Who we are

Your home is the setting. We bring the support.

Sonder Homecare is a Minnesota home and community based services provider licensed under DHS chapter 245D. We don't run a facility. Our caregivers go where our clients already live: their own homes, a family member's home, and the community around them.

Each client has a person-centered plan built around their own goals and routines, and the same caregivers show up week after week. Families and case managers get updates without having to ask for them. Our office is in Bloomington; our work happens at your address.

FAQ

Common questions.

What families and referring professionals ask most. If yours isn't here, ask us.

Get in touch
Where do services happen?+

In the client's own home, a family member's home, or out in the community. We don't operate a facility or group home. Our office is in Bloomington; our caregivers come to you.

What is a 245D license, and why does it matter?+

Chapter 245D is the Minnesota DHS licensing standard for home and community based services. It sets requirements for staff training, person-centered planning, documentation, and rights protections. A 245D license means the state audits how we operate.

How is care paid for?+

Most clients are funded through Minnesota's Medicaid waiver programs, including CADI, BI, DD, and the Elderly Waiver, coordinated through a county or tribal case manager. If you're not sure where to start, we can point you to the right first call.

How do I refer a client?+

Send us the referral form on the contact section, or reach out directly with the client's support needs and waiver information. We reply within one business day and tell you quickly whether we're a fit.

Do you provide residential or group home care?+

No. We don't provide community residential services, crisis respite, integrated community supports, or ICF/DD services. Our work is in-home and community-based: IHS, respite, night supervision, homemaker services, and most other basic and intensive 245D services.

How do you work with hospitals and physicians?+

We coordinate with discharge planners so in-home support is arranged before the patient leaves, then keep treating providers updated after the transition, so discharges home actually hold.

How do you keep families and case managers informed?+

Progress reports go out on schedule, incidents get reported the way 245D requires, and in between you can just call.

Careers

Work that means something.

We hire people who treat caregiving as a profession. Four roles are open now. Every role requires a passed background check.

Direct Support Professional

$18–$25 / hr

The heart of the agency. DSPs work one-on-one with clients in their homes and communities. We hire experienced caregivers on purpose: they train fast, and the quality of care shows it.

What you'll do

  • Daily living support and skill building in clients' homes
  • Community outings and appointments
  • Shift documentation in 245D format

What you'll need

  • High school diploma
  • 2+ years caregiving for people with intellectual disabilities or on a waiver plan
  • Pass a background check

Designated Coordinator / Designated Manager

$25–$35 / hr

The DC/DM oversees service delivery and compliance for the whole agency, and is the county's go-to person at Sonder.

What you'll do

  • Complete intake paperwork
  • Write abuse prevention plans and support plans
  • Work directly with case managers and the county

What you'll need

  • Bachelor's degree with 5 years of experience, or master's with 3
  • Pass a background check

Case Manager

$25–$30 / hr

Our families' advocate. You coordinate with county case managers so nothing about a client's care falls through the cracks.

What you'll need

  • Bachelor's degree
  • Pass a background check

$30–$50 / hr
