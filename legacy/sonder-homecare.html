Our services

Everything under our 245D license

Listed the way your case manager or support plan names them, with what each one actually means for your family.

License category 1

Basic Support Services

Day-to-day support that keeps life at home safe and steady.

  • 24-hour emergency assistance Someone to call and a plan that responds, any hour, any day.
  • Adult companion services Company for meals, errands, hobbies, and time in the community.
  • Homemaker Help keeping the household running: cleaning, laundry, meal prep.
  • Night supervision Overnight staff in the home so everyone can actually sleep.
  • Respite care Short-term relief for family caregivers, in home or out of home.
  • Individual community living support Support for older adults to keep living in their own home.
  • Individualized home supports Help with daily living in the person's own home, on their terms.

License category 2

In-Home Support Services

Skill-building services that grow independence over time.

  • Semi-independent living skills Practicing budgeting, cooking, transportation, and self-advocacy toward more independent living.
  • Individualized home supports with training Daily-living support plus structured teaching of new skills, tracked against the person's own goals.
  • Individualized home supports with family training The same skill-building, with the family trained alongside so progress holds between visits.

Getting started

From first call to first visit

Whether you're a parent, a guardian, or a county case manager, the path in is the same three steps.

Step one

Talk with us

Tell us about the person who needs support, the services in their plan, and what a good week looks like for them. No forms required to start the conversation.

Step two

Build the support plan

We meet the person and their team, confirm services and authorization with the county, and write a plan around their goals, not a template.

Step three

Start care at home

Matched staff begin visits on a schedule that fits the household. We review progress with you and adjust as life changes.
